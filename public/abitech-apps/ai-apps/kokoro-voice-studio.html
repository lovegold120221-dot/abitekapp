Kokoro Voice Studio

Type anything — an 82M-parameter voice model reads it aloud, entirely on your device.

Loading Kokoro-82M… WebAssembly

Runs onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX locally via kokoro-js + transformers.js. The first run downloads ~90 MB of model weights, then it works offline. Nothing you type leaves your device.
